This talk explores the rapidly evolving interplay between quantum computing and artificial intelligence, structured around the themes Quantum for AI and AI for Quantum. 
We begin by introducing the principles of quantum computing and quantum algorithms, followed by near-term quantum machine learning approaches for both predictive and generative modeling. We then shift to how AI accelerates quantum science and technology, including quantum simulation and quantum error correction. Emphasizing hybrid quantum-classical architectures, the talk highlights emerging opportunities, practical challenges, and the path toward quantum-enhanced AI and AI-enhanced quantum technology.
